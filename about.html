⚡ What "IWAJU" Means
In the Yoruba language, "Iwaju" translates to "Forward", "The Forefront", and "The Vanguard". We chose this name because we exist to propel Africa's commercial heartbeat forward. For decades, micro, small, and medium enterprises (MSMEs) have generated 80% of employment across the continent yet remain locked out of traditional credit because they lack audited balance sheets or title deeds.
